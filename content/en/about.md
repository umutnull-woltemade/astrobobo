# About Astrobobo

Astrobobo was built to deliver accurate, AI-readable, and warm guidance on astrology, dream interpretation, and inner exploration. Our goal is to translate technical precision into everyday language and give readers the power to interpret their own experiences.

## Our Mission

We combine the ancient wisdom of traditional astrology with Swiss Ephemeris-grade calculations. Our content is definition-first, written in short sentences, and free of judgmental language. Every article passes editorial review; we accept no spam, clickbait, or unfounded claims.

## Editorial Principles

- **Definition comes first.** Every article tells you what it covers in the first three sentences.
- **Respect for evidence.** Astrological calculations use Swiss Ephemeris.
- **Personal exploration.** Content is never a substitute for psychological, medical, or religious advice.
- **Transparent language.** When uncertainty exists we say "may," not "definitely."
- **Continuous updating.** Our articles are reviewed on a rolling basis.
